1. Intended use

Sequmio is a QA tool for launching and testing Android applications that you own or have permission to test. You must comply with applicable laws, Google Play rules, and third-party app terms. Do not use Sequmio to misrepresent genuine testing or manipulate review systems.

2. Test-stage software

The app is provided during testing. Runs may stop because of Android permissions, system restrictions or device battery policies. A completed run is not a guarantee of Google Play approval.

3. Accounts and data

Local Runner does not require an account. Optional Google sign-in, when enabled, uses Firebase to store your basic account profile. Test plans, histories and My App drafts stay local unless you explicitly share a report. See our Privacy Policy.

4. Community features

App List, Join and shared scenarios require a configured Firebase backend. The tester personally completes Google Group and Play opt-in steps. Sequmio cannot verify those enrollments or guarantee Play review approval. All automated UI scenarios are deterministic and initiated by the tester.

5. Support

For technical or data requests, contact the developer via Sequmio support issues.
