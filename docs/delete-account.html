Sequmio — Delete your account

Sequmio by GIVLSE gives users control over their Google/Firebase-linked testing account.

Delete from the Android app

  1. Open Sequmio and tap the settings gear.
  2. Open Profile and sign in if requested.
  3. Choose Delete my Sequmio account and confirm.
  4. Sequmio attempts to remove your profile, published apps, tester memberships, and Firebase authentication account. Reauthentication may be required for security.

Request deletion without the app

You can initiate a deletion request through Sequmio developer support. Use the issue title Sequmio account deletion request. Do not post your email address, authentication tokens, personal data or passwords in a public issue. The developer will need a secure identity-verification method before processing the request.

What gets deleted

Your Firebase profile, owned app listings, your tester membership records, submitted/owned QA run reports, daily digests, scenario version snapshots and your Firebase Auth account. Test plans, app drafts and history stored only on a device can be removed by clearing Sequmio storage or uninstalling the app. Third-party Google accounts and Google Groups memberships are not deleted by Sequmio.

If an operation fails, no success is claimed. Report the error through support. The developer must operate and verify this deletion-request process before enabling public accounts.
