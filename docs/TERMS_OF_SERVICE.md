# Sequmio Terms of Service

**Effective:** November 24, 2026

**Scope.** Sequmio is a QA utility that launches user-selected Android applications for testing. Local Runner remains available without an online account. Community functions and shared scenarios are planned but are not active in V0.5.

**Responsibility.** Only test applications you own, have permission to test, or are authorized to access. Do not use Sequmio to misrepresent genuine user engagement, manipulate platform reviews or bypass rules of Google Play or third-party services.

**Your data.** My App drafts and run history stay on your device unless you explicitly share a report. If you opt into Google sign-in after its backend is configured, Firebase stores your basic account profile as described in the [Privacy Policy](https://givlse.github.io/AppCycleTester/privacy.html).

**Availability.** This is test-stage software. Android and OEM battery/permissions policies can interrupt runs. Successful QA runs do not guarantee Google Play review approval.

**Third-party links.** Google Groups, Play opt-in and Play Store links, when available in future versions, are controlled by their owners and subject to their respective terms.

**Support.** Contact the developer through https://github.com/GIVLSE/AppCycleTester/issues.

**Automated E2E playback.** Developers must publish only deterministic, authorized test scenarios. Do not include account passwords, payment steps, personal information or instructions to consent to system permissions without human action. Other testers initiate test execution themselves and may stop it.
